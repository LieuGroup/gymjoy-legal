A calm, friendly workout companion for iPhone. No account needed. Your workouts and photos stay on your phone.

Support

Questions, feedback, a privacy request or trouble with the app? Email us at oscar.lehuu@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few working days.

  • Manage or cancel a subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple.
  • Delete your data: in GymJoy, open Settings to delete your photos or all of your data. Deleting the app also removes the data stored on your phone.
