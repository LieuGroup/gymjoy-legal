1. Who we are

GymJoy is an iPhone app provided by LilGroup ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it is kept and the choices you have. It applies to the GymJoy iOS app and this website.

For users in the EU, EEA and UK, LilGroup is the "controller" of the limited personal data we process. You can reach us at oscar.lehuu@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your iPhone

GymJoy works without an account. The things you create in the app stay on your device:

  • workout logs and training history;
  • your levels, streaks and gems;
  • check-in photos you take or choose in the app;
  • your app settings.

This data is stored locally on your iPhone. We do not upload it to our servers and we cannot access it.

iCloud sync. If you turn on iCloud sync, this data is also stored in your own private iCloud account (private CloudKit database) so it can sync between your Apple devices. That storage is provided by Apple under your agreement with Apple. We cannot access your iCloud data.

The age, height, weight and sex we read from Apple Health (see below) are used on your iPhone only and are not included in iCloud sync or in any data we or our providers receive.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

GymJoy Pro is sold through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes your payment. We never receive your card details, your Apple ID, your name or your email address.

We use RevenueCat to check whether your Pro subscription or purchase is active. To do that, the app gives RevenueCat an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID together with your App Store purchase information (for example which product you bought, when, the price and the store receipt) and basic technical data such as device model, OS version, app version, country and IP address. RevenueCat does not receive your name or email.

Refunds, billing questions and cancellations are handled by Apple. See our Terms of Use for subscription details.

4. Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional. If you give permission, GymJoy reads the following from Apple Health to personalise your plan:

  • age (date of birth);
  • height;
  • weight;
  • biological sex.

GymJoy never writes any data back to Apple Health. Health data is used only on your iPhone to provide app features. It is never sent to our servers, never included in analytics events, never included in a Future You request, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold and never shared with data brokers. We follow Apple's HealthKit rules and the App Store Review Guidelines for health data.

You can change or withdraw access at any time in the iPhone Settings app (Privacy & Security, then Health, then GymJoy) or in the Health app. Withdrawing access does not affect the rest of the app.

5. Future You (AI image feature)

Future You is an optional Pro feature for people aged 18 or over. It creates an AI illustration of how you might look as you keep training. It only runs if you turn it on and give explicit consent in the app. You can say no and still use everything else in GymJoy.

How it works

  • Before your first use, we tell you exactly which company will receive your photo and why, and ask you to give explicit permission. Nothing is sent until you agree.
  • When you ask for a Future You image, the app sends one check-in photo you choose over an encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connection to a small relay server we run (a Cloudflare Worker). That server does not store the photo; it passes it straight through.
  • The relay server passes the photo to our AI image provider, fal.ai, which creates AI-edited pictures of you at 3, 6, 9 and 12 months, clearly labelled "AI visualisation".
  • The generated images are sent back to your iPhone and saved there.
  • The copy of your photo sent to fal.ai, and the generated images, are deleted at fal.ai within 24 hours. Our relay server keeps no copy at any point. Your original photo and the saved results stay on your iPhone.

Sensitive data and consent

A body photo may reveal information about your body shape or health. We treat these photos as sensitive ("special category") data and only send one to fal.ai with your explicit, separate consent, given on a dedicated screen in the app before the first upload, naming fal.ai as the recipient. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off in Settings. Withdrawing consent stops future uploads; it does not affect images already created.

Model training

fal.ai's standard terms say it does not use API inputs to train its own general-purpose models; we have not independently audited this. We do not use your photos or generated images to train any model ourselves.

We do not use your photo for facial recognition or to identify you, and neither we nor fal.ai match it against any other photo or database.

An illustration, not a prediction

A Future You image is an artistic AI illustration for motivation, generated by artificial intelligence and labelled as such. It is not a medical, health or fitness prediction and it does not show results you will achieve.

6. Anonymous analytics

To understand which features are used and to fix problems, GymJoy sends pseudonymous usage analytics to PostHog (PostHog, Inc., United States; data stored in the US). Events include things like which screen was opened, a workout being completed, or the onboarding and paywall steps you passed through, together with basic technical details such as app version and iOS version. The app creates a random analytics ID on your iPhone that is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID. We have configured PostHog not to build personal profiles, not to store your IP address and not to record your screen. These events do not include your name, email, photos, workout notes or Apple Health data, and they are not used to track you across other apps or websites.

We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), we do not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, and we do not use analytics for advertising. If you are in the EU, EEA or UK, analytics stays off until you agree to it in the app. Everyone can turn analytics off at any time in Settings → Share anonymous usage data; the app then stops sending events.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply and help you, and we delete the conversation within 12 months of your last message unless we need it for longer to deal with a legal claim or regulatory request.

8. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not "share" your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We do not show third-party ads in GymJoy.
  • We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across apps and websites.
  • We do not use Apple Health data for advertising, marketing or any purpose other than providing GymJoy.
  • We do not use your photos to train AI models.

10. How long we keep data

  • On your iPhone and in your iCloud: until you delete it in the app, delete the app, or remove it from iCloud.
  • Future You uploads and results at fal.ai: deleted automatically within 24 hours; our relay server stores no copy at any point.
  • Purchase records at RevenueCat and Apple: for as long as your subscription is active, plus the period we must keep financial records for tax and accounting law (typically several years).
  • Anonymous analytics: kept by PostHog for up to 12 months, then deleted.
  • Support emails: up to 12 months after your last message, then deleted.

11. Deleting your data and your choices

  • Delete photos: in GymJoy, open Settings and delete all photos.
  • Delete everything: in GymJoy, open Settings and delete all data. Because there is no account, deleting the app also removes the data stored on your iPhone.
  • iCloud: if you turned on iCloud sync, using "delete all data" in the app also removes your synced iCloud copy, because that is the same private CloudKit database. If you delete the app instead, data may remain in your iCloud account until you remove it in your iPhone's iCloud settings.
  • Apple Health: turn off access in the iPhone Settings or Health app at any time.
  • Future You: turn the feature off in Settings. The fal.ai copy is deleted automatically within 24 hours regardless.
  • Anything else: email oscar.lehuu@gmail.com.

Because we do not have accounts and hold almost no data that identifies you, we may not be able to find records linked to you at RevenueCat or PostHog from an email address alone. Tell us the approximate date and device so we can try to locate the anonymous ID; if we cannot find a match we will tell you, and most of your data can in any case be deleted directly on your device.

12. Age limits and children

GymJoy is intended for people aged 13 and over, matching its App Store age rating. Future You and other photo-based AI features are only for people aged 18 and over; we check this using Apple's Declared Age Range where available, or your self-declared birth year, before letting you use them.

GymJoy is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used a photo feature, contact us and we will help delete any data we hold.

13. Security

Data on your iPhone is protected by iOS security, including your passcode and device encryption. Future You photos travel over encrypted connections and are deleted from fal.ai within 24 hours; our relay server stores no copy at any point. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the data we handle to a minimum to reduce risk.

14. International transfers

Some of our providers, including RevenueCat, PostHog and fal.ai, are based in the United States, so a Future You photo and purchase information may be processed there; our relay server runs on Cloudflare's global network, which may also process a photo briefly in the US while forwarding it. When personal data from the EU, EEA or UK is transferred to the US, we rely on the safeguards our providers offer under GDPR Chapter V, in particular the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement for UK data) or, where a provider is certified, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
